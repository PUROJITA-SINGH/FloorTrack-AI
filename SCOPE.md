# SCOPE

## MVP boundary
- MVP boundary: one floor, infusion pumps only. Everything else (multi-floor, multi-asset-type, real detectors, AoA/phase ranging, ML smoothing, live hospital integrations) is deferred to T2.

## Claims discipline
- Confidence score is an uncalibrated weighting of evidence, NOT a probability.
- Shortage indicator is a normalized shortfall ratio, calibrated=false.
- Never claim: 90% cost reduction, sub-2-min search time, room-level/sub-metre accuracy, 12 cm position error, fleet-size reduction, payback period, or any probability on confidence score.

## Three tiers
- T0 (36-hour demo)
- T1 (3-week build)
- T2 (4-week pilot-ready)
- This sprint targets 80% of T1/T2 critical path.
